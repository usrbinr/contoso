The business reports

contoso includes 118 fictional internal business reports about Contoso’s 2025 trading. They are the documents a finance team reads alongside its sales data: weekly flashes, monthly reviews, quarterly country reports, committee minutes and plan memos. Each is written by a named person in a named business unit, published on a realistic date, and based on the “mega” dataset from create_contoso_duckdb("mega").

reports <- read_reports()

Dimensions of a report

Metadata

Every report carries the same eight metadata fields, plus its text:

Field Type Example
id text sales-flash-2025-w20
title text Weekly Sales Flash - Week 20 (12 May 2025 to 18 May 2025)
unit text Sales Operations
author text Priya Raman, Director of Sales Operations
report_type text sales_flash
period_start date 2025-05-12
period_end date 2025-05-18
published date 2025-05-19
body Markdown text the report itself

period_start and period_end give the period a report covers, and published gives the date it was released. The gap between them varies by type: a weekly flash comes out the day after its week ends, a regional review about 12 days after its quarter. The plan memo is negative: it is published in December 2024, before the year it covers.

Business dimensions

The figures in each report are cut by the same dimensions as the contoso data. Which cuts a report uses depends on its type:

Report type Period Breakdowns Compared with
sales_flash Week Total, channel (online / stores), country, category Prior week, same week last year
regional Quarter One country: total and category Prior quarter, last year, budget
ecommerce Month Channel mix, online total, online by category Prior month, last year, budget
supply_chain Month Total, manufacturer, category Prior month, last year, budget
manufacturing Month Contoso, Ltd products: total, category, product line Prior month, last year
pricing The 8 weeks before the meeting Total, category, channel Prior 8 weeks, last year
aop Full year 2025 Budget by channel, country, category and month 2024 actuals
alert One event Vendors, categories and regions affected None
industry Quarter Market-level demand by category and channel (no Contoso figures) Market last year

The values those breakdowns take:

Dimension Values
Channel Online, Stores
Country United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands, Australia, plus Online
Category Computers, Cell phones, Home Appliances, TV and Video, Cameras and camcorders, Audio, Games and Toys, Music, Movies and Audio Books
Manufacturer 11, including Contoso, Ltd, the company’s own private label

Measures

The numbers quoted in the reports are these measures:

Measure Meaning
Net revenue Sales after discounts, in USD
Change vs prior period Against the previous week, month or quarter
Change vs last year Against the same period a year earlier
Variance to budget Against the 2025 Annual Operating Plan
Gross margin % Gross margin as a share of net revenue
Discount % Discounts as a share of gross revenue
Cost ratio Cost of goods as a share of gross revenue
Units Units sold

Together they tell one story about 2025: an exit from third-party marketplaces, a TV range reset, a store refit programme (“Store Next”), a product recall, a PC refresh, and the usual stocktake, summer shutdown and holiday season. Each author only knows what their unit would know on the publication date.

Some reports are deliberately wrong. A few authors misread the data or credit the wrong cause, as real business writing does. The package does not say which, so an FP&A assistant has to find them by checking the reports against the data.
